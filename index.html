How Flipbook works

        
      

      

        
        

          
Drawing

          
          
    
            
  • The tools are on the left: Brush, Eraser, Lasso and Fill.
    • 
            
  • Tap the circle for Thickness, or press it and drag up or down.
    • 
            
  • Tap the colored square for the Color picker. "Pick from drawing" takes a color from the picture.
    • 
            
  • With an Apple Pencil, the pencil draws and fingers move the page.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Frames

          
          
    
            
  • The strip at the bottom shows your frames. Tap one to go to it, or drag the strip.
    • 
            
  • + adds a new frame after the current one.
    • 
            
  • Tap the current frame for Duplicate, Delete and Hold (show it for longer).
    • 
            
  • Clear empties the drawing but keeps the frame.
    • 
            
  • Play shows the animation. Tap the screen to stop.
    • 
            
  • Turn on Onion in the menu to see the frame before in red and the one after in green.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Layers and pictures

          
          
    
            
  • Tap the layer name beside the frames to add, hide, reorder or rename layers.
    • 
            
  • Image puts a picture on the current frame, on a layer of its own.
    • 
            
  • Project settings in the menu sets the background color or a background picture for every frame, and the speed.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Moving and copying

          
          
    
            
  • With the Lasso, draw around something. Drag it to move, drag a corner to resize, drag the round handle to turn.
    • 
            
  • Copy and Paste work on the selection, or on the whole frame when nothing is selected.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Fingers and keys

          
          
    
            
  • Pinch with two fingers to zoom, drag with two fingers to move. The corner button fits the page to the screen.
    • 
            
  • Tap with two fingers to undo, with three to redo.
    • 
            
  • Press and hold any button to see what it does.
    • 
            
  • Keyboard: arrows change frame and layer, Space plays, O onion, E eraser, B brush.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Keeping your work

          
          
    
            
  • Everything is saved on this device as you draw. Nothing is sent anywhere.
    • 
            
  • Add Flipbook to the Home Screen so the browser never clears your drawings.
    • 
            
  • Save project file in the menu makes a backup you can keep in Files. Open it again from Projects.
    • 
            
  • Make movie saves a video or GIF to share.
